# Locks and transactions

- In `prod` no session waits longer than `lock_timeout` (10 s, topic "Security"); scheduled purges run on a pool of two (`spring.task.scheduling.pool.size`), so one waiting purge never stalls the others.
- Lock order: actor account → destination clubs by ID → referenced glazes by ID → combinations by ID → image rows by ID; keep it across FK writes and audits. Account and club mutations lock the row.
- A manufacturer row is locked last: a glaze removal locks it after the glaze and its images and checks for remaining glazes under that lock; a creation under an existing manufacturer locks it after the stock owner (`GlazeRemovalLockApiTest`).
- Two accounts (buddy writes and a ban): both rows in `BuddyId.ORDER` (the database's unsigned byte order of UUIDs, not `UUID.compareTo`), whichever is the actor; then at most the club of a member request (buddy), or the banned account's clubs and the rest of the deletion's order (ban). No other write locks a second account.
- Nothing locks an account row after a club (an insert's FK key-share under a club lock does not conflict, see `FOR UPDATE` below).
- New or edited steps prelock their glazes before the parent; a variation's source row is locked after the step glazes.
- Membership and role decisions that gate a club write are read after the club lock, as scalars or the transaction's first load of that `Membership` (`ClubService.lockAdminClub`, `ClubService.removeMember`, `StockService.lockOwner` and `remove` (role and adder), `ImageController.lockClub`, `WishlistService.lockClub` (role, then the entry and its adder), the club-admin role in `ImageController.lockManaged` (photo deletion and type change) via `MembershipRepository.roleOf`; a join locks the club by invite hash).
- Membership and role decisions that gate a club write are read after the club lock, as scalars or the transaction's first load of that `Membership` (`ClubService.lockAdminClub`, `ClubService.removeMember`, `StockService.lockOwner` and `remove` (role and adder), `ImageController.lockClub`, the club-admin role in `ImageController.lockManaged` (photo deletion, type and focus changes) via `MembershipRepository.roleOf`; a join locks the club by invite hash).
  - A check before the lock is only an early refusal and is repeated under it (`ClubMembershipLockApiTest`). Every club write makes that early check, so a non-member never takes the club lock (topic "Authorization and visibility").
  - Every write that depends on a membership (stock, club uploads and visibility changes, the author check of `requireVisibleToAuthor`) reads it after that club's lock: it sees a leave committed before and is seen by one queued after (`CombinationConcurrencyApiTest`, PostgreSQL).
  - Read-only listings need no club lock.
- Leaving and removal lock the actor's account, then the club, never the removed member's account (their account deletion locks account → club, so club → that account could deadlock). Actor role, target membership and admin count are read under the club lock; the membership goes by a bulk delete, which a concurrent pending purge (account lock only) may already have done.
- Never lock accounts `FOR UPDATE`: a club stock or glaze insert under the club lock key-share-locks the actor's account row through its FK while a leave or account deletion holds that row and waits for the club. This works only because Hibernate's PESSIMISTIC_WRITE is `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` on PostgreSQL (no conflict with key share).
- Uploads encode and store outside DB locks, then lock account, destination club (membership rechecked), target and image insertion in one transaction; failed DB work removes both new objects (full size and card).
- Visibility changes use a scalar target/uploader preflight (never a preloaded managed image), lock the destination club before parent and image, then re-lock the image and recheck ownership, PUBLIC ID rotation included.
- The sync change log adds no lock: `ChangeRecorder` inserts in `beforeCommit` after a repository flush (the last statement of every recording transaction), and the table has no foreign or unique key but its identity, so the insert never waits. The epoch is derived (topic "Sync"): leaving, removal, joining and role changes write nothing for the sync and never touch the removed member's row.
- Account deletion (and a ban, after its two account locks) locks the account → its clubs → authored glazes and combinations by ID → image rows (one ID-ordered query for the user's and the removed clubs' images).
  - It collects club IDs as scalars so memberships and roles load only under the club lock (a managed `Membership` keeps a stale role).
  - It need not lock external parents to remove or anonymise their photos.
- Audit-writing removals lock the actor and the image rows before snapshotting metadata.
- `login_session` rows come after the account:
  - password change and reset delete them under the account lock, account deletion by cascade with the account row;
  - renewal and sign-out change one row by key in their own transaction and lock nothing else;
  - a sign-in's insert only key-shares the account row (never blocked by `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`). It waits for an account deletion or ban and then fails its foreign key, which `LoginSessions.saveContext` turns into a `BadCredentialsException`: form login answers the wrong-password 401 with no session cookie, a `SessionLogin.signIn` caller (magic link, password change) the `SIGN_IN_REQUIRED` 401; never a 500 or 409.
- `ImageRemovalService` removes storage objects only after commit with the transaction context suspended (NOT_SUPPORTED); failures warn and never undo committed records; a rollback never removes objects. `deleteObjectsAfterCommit` is that step alone (profile pictures, the evidence purge and feedback use it).
- Profile pictures: every write locks the acting account first, then the `user_avatar` row or, once the picture is gone, its `avatar_evidence` row, and touches reports, evidence and blocks only after that (topic "Profile pictures"). No other lockable row is taken after them, so they fit after any prefix of the order above.
