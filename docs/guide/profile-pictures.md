# Profile pictures

- Rule (product decision; the privacy statement and the terms `terms.section.profilePicture` say the same): at most one per account, shown to EVERY signed-in, verified account and never to guests; it may show the account holder, never another person (moderation by report).
- Not a photo: its own table `user_avatar` (`uq_user_avatar_user`, `uq_user_avatar_key`, `fk_user_avatar_user` ON DELETE CASCADE) and endpoints; no `ImageKind`, no visibility; never in `ImageRecord.visibleTo`, a photo listing, the deletion log or the sync.
- Shared with photos is only the lower layer:
  - `ImageEncoder.encodeAvatar`: the photo limits, content sniffing, single-encode permit and metadata stripping; the centre square, upright, at most `AVATAR_SIDE` 256 px, never upscaled, plus a copy of at most `AVATAR_SMALL_SIDE` 64 px from the same encode. Never trust the client's crop: any other shape is cut to its centre.
  - storage keys (`<uuid>.jpg` the square, `ImageStorage.cardKey` the small copy), `ImageController.storeObjects` (both objects before the transaction, both removed when it fails), `ImageRemovalService.deleteObjectsAfterCommit`, `ImageController.browserOnly`.

## Endpoints

- `AvatarController`: all signed-in (guests 401; `SessionVersionFilter` signs out unverified accounts); writes need CSRF and the current terms, except the owner's own removal.
  - `POST /api/avatars` (multipart `file`) sets or replaces the caller's picture → `IdView`; 409 `AvatarService.PICTURE_REFUSED` for a blocked picture.
  - `DELETE /api/avatars/{id}`: its owner (also with pending terms) or a site admin (moderation); 403 `REMOVE_RIGHTS_REQUIRED` for any other account (428 first with pending terms); 404 `AVATAR_NOT_FOUND` when gone.
  - `GET /api/avatars/{id}` and `/small`: any signed-in account, current pictures only.
  - `POST /api/avatars/{id}/reports` `{reason}` (`ImageReportInput`, as for photo reports): once per reporter, ever (`uq_avatar_report_reporter`; a repeat is 204 and keeps the first, a closed one too); 400 `OWN_AVATAR_REPORT` for the owner.
  - A separate queue, not `image_report`: the photo queue's `imageId` stays non-null for deployed clients.
- Site admins (401 guests, 403 others; writes need CSRF and the current terms):
  - `GET /api/admin/avatar-reports?page=&status=` → `AvatarReportPageView {entries, hasMore}` of `AvatarReportView`: newest first, the photo queue's paging and page size, one slice query; `status` (`OPEN`/`DISMISSED`/`ACTIONED`/`CLOSED`, the last without a decision: account gone or expired) narrows it. `avatarId` is the same for every report of one picture; `owner`/`reporter`/`closedBy` are current display names (null once that account is deleted or nobody closed it); `picture` is `CURRENT`, `REPLACED`, `OWNER_REMOVED`, `ADMIN_REMOVED` or `ACCOUNT_DELETED`; `hasPicture` says whether the picture endpoint can show it.
  - `GET /api/admin/avatar-reports/{id}/picture`: the reported 256 px square (the current picture while `CURRENT`, else its kept evidence) as `image/jpeg` with `Cache-Control: no-store, private` and `X-Accel-Expires: 0`, so no browser or proxy keeps a copy and a purge is final. 404 `REPORT_NOT_FOUND` for an unknown report, 404 `AVATAR_NOT_FOUND` once purged or never kept.
  - `POST …/{id}/dismiss` and `POST …/{id}/remove` → 204; they decide for EVERY open report of that report's picture: DISMISSED (the picture stays) or ACTIONED (removed if still current, blocked either way, also after a replacement or the owner's account deletion). The same decision again is a 204 no-op; the other decision on a closed report is 409 `CHANGED_MEANWHILE` (reports never reopen; to act on a dismissed picture that is still current use `DELETE /api/avatars/{id}`); 404 `REPORT_NOT_FOUND`.
  - `DELETE /api/avatars/{id}` by a site admin on another account's picture equals `remove` (blocked, open reports ACTIONED), with or without reports; logged by IDs only.

## Reports, evidence and blocks

- Reports (`avatar_report`, `model/AvatarReport`): no foreign key to the picture, so a report outlives a replacement, a removal and the owner's account deletion. `status` (`ck_avatar_report_status`, `ck_avatar_report_closed`) with `closed_at`/`closed_by` (`fk_avatar_report_closed_by` ON DELETE SET NULL). `owner_id` is the picture's account at report time (`fk_avatar_report_owner` ON DELETE SET NULL; set on every report, so null means the owner's account is gone); the reporter's likewise.
  - Expiry (product decision): the daily `AvatarService.expireReports` closes reports still OPEN `glazelab.moderation.report-expiry` (90 days) after their opening as `CLOSED` (no `closed_by`; both decisions then answer 409), one picture per transaction under its picture or evidence row lock, then runs the evidence purge. A CLOSED report never keeps evidence: only OPEN reports and decisions within the retention do (`retire`, `AvatarEvidenceRepository.expired`).
  - Reports are never deleted (product decision). When the owner's account goes (deletion, ban, pending purge) `AvatarService.removeOf` closes its open reports as `CLOSED` (no decision, no `closed_by`; both decisions then answer 409) and nulls the `reason` of every report about its pictures (it may name the owner; product decision). A reporter's own deletion keeps their reports' text.
- Evidence (`avatar_evidence`, `model/AvatarEvidence`, keyed by the picture's ID): whenever a picture that has reports leaves its account (replaced, removed by owner or admin, account deleted), `AvatarService.retire` writes how it went, its three hashes and, while one of its reports is OPEN or was closed within `glazelab.avatar.evidence-retention` (90 days, promised by the terms), the storage key (a CLOSED report keeps none). An unreported picture leaves nothing behind.
  - The objects stay in place, but no `user_avatar` row names them: no public or user endpoint can reach them (`GET /api/avatars/{id}` reads `user_avatar` only); only the admin picture endpoint reads the key.
  - The daily `AvatarService.purgeEvidence` clears the key and deletes the objects after commit once no report of the picture is open and none was decided within the retention (a conditional update per row, so it races nothing; logs counts only); the row keeps outcome and hashes. It also releases evidence whose reports lost their owner.
  - Account deletion, a ban and the pending purge (product decision) delete the current picture and every evidence copy of the account's earlier pictures at once (found through `avatar_report.owner_id`, `AvatarEvidenceRepository.keptOf`; `retire` never keeps an `ACCOUNT_DELETED` picture; keys released in ID order, objects after commit). The fingerprints stay (the evidence row's hashes, every block), so a blocked picture stays blocked for every account.
  - Evidence and blocks have no account column. A ban with `blockPictures` decides first (topic "Bans"), then the same deletion runs.
- Blocks (`avatar_block`, `model/AvatarBlock`): hashes only, no account, no picture ID, no expiry. Written when a site admin removes a picture (`moderate`), actions a gone one or bans its account with `blockPictures` (each logged in `admin_action`); skipped when the same exact pair is blocked already (no unique key: a racing duplicate is harmless).
- `model/AvatarFingerprint` per upload: SHA-256 of the uploaded bytes, SHA-256 of the stored square, and a 64-bit dHash of the stored square (`TIE_MARGIN` keeps flat areas from flipping with every re-encode).
- `AvatarService.refuseBlocked` runs on every upload before the objects are stored, for every account, and refuses with one generic 409 that names no report or account when either exact hash equals either blocked exact hash, or both dHashes are informative (at least `MIN_INFORMATIVE_BITS` 8 set and 8 unset bits) and differ in at most `glazelab.avatar.block-distance` bits (default 8, inclusive).
  - Catches re-encodes, rescales and re-crops of about 2 % of the side. Cannot catch larger re-crops, mirrored, rotated, recoloured, filtered, edited or re-photographed copies, or an almost plain picture beyond its exact copy (uninformative hash).
  - False positives: simple graphics of the same layout can hash within 8 bits. The refusal does not say why; the operator lifts a block with an SQL delete by its hashes (`avatar_evidence` keeps them).
  - Existing pictures are never scanned, and an upload racing the removal that blocks it is not refused (no lock; it can be reported).
  - It holds every block in memory per upload (one statement): fine for hundreds, revisit at many thousands.

## Caching, views and locks

- The picture's ID is its URL and is new for every picture (a replacement deletes the old row), so an old URL answers 404 at once and the success answer can be `private, max-age=604800, immutable` (`ImageController.browserOnly`, the CLUB/PRIVATE photo week) with `X-Accel-Expires: 0`; errors keep Spring Security's `no-store`. Nginx never stores one (topic "Contract").
- Views (per viewer, never answered to a guest): `UserView.avatarId` (own), `MemberView.avatarId` (`MembershipRepository.membersOf`, one statement), `BuddyView.avatarId` (`BuddyRepository.of`), `BuddyLinkPreviewView.avatarId` (the owner's picture, still no owner ID; accepted: the avatar ID can be linked to a user ID through `author-avatars` or a shared member list, which only tells the requester what the request answers anyway).
- Authors: `CombinationView` (a synced stored view) never carries a picture, so `SyncService.FORMAT` and `VIEW_SHAPES` stay and an avatar change re-sends nothing. `GET /api/combinations/author-avatars` answers `[{userId, avatarId}]` for every account that authored a published combination (`UserAvatarRepository.ofAuthors`, one statement, signed-in only, no per-viewer filter: published combinations and their `authorId` are public), fetched in full; a stale ID meanwhile is a 404. Writes are `EXEMPT` in `SyncCoverageApiTest`.
- Locks: account → picture row → (reports, evidence, blocks).
  - A replacement locks its account, then its current picture (`lockByUserId`), retires it (reads its reports, deletes the row by ID, writes evidence), then inserts.
  - A removal and a report lock the actor's account, then the picture row (`lockById`): a report queued behind a replacement or removal finds it gone (404); one ahead of it is seen and keeps the picture as evidence.
  - A decision locks the admin's account, then the picture row, or its evidence row once it is gone, and reads the report's status under that lock.
  - Account deletion and the pending purge retire the picture in `AccountRows`, after the deletion's row locks (only the feedback delete follows); they release evidence rows in ID order after the picture row and update the reports last. The evidence purge releases in ID order too, so they cannot deadlock; the report expiry locks one picture or evidence row per transaction, then its reports.
  - A replacement waiting behind its account's deletion fails its account lock (401) and removes the objects it wrote (`AvatarConcurrencyApiTest`, PostgreSQL).
